## Application Form Handling Rules

- Answer factual questions using my confirmed profile, resume, and approved answers.
- Do not invent work experience, years of experience, certifications, salary figures, or technical proficiency.
- For visa sponsorship, citizenship, security clearance, and legal eligibility, ask for confirmation whenever the answer is unknown.
- For salary questions, use a specific range only when I have explicitly approved one.
- For relocation and work arrangements, use the approved preferences in my profile.
- If an answer cannot be determined confidently, flag the question for my review rather than guessing.
